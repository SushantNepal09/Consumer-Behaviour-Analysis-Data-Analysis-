 # Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** analyzing 3,900 customer transactions to understand shopping behavior, spending patterns, customer segments, product performance, discounts, subscriptions, and shipping preferences.

The project uses **Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI** to transform raw customer shopping data into business insights and actionable recommendations.

> **Business Question:**
> How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

---

##  Project Overview

A retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve **sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty**.

The analysis focuses on purchasing patterns across:

* Customer demographics
* Product categories
* Subscription status
* Purchase history
* Discounts
* Product ratings
* Shipping preferences
* Age groups
* Customer segments

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

```text

     ↓

     ↓

     ↓
PostgreSQL
     ↓

     ↓
Power BI Dashboard
     ↓
Business Insights & Recommendations
     ↓
Report & Presentation
```

The project analyzes **3,900 transactions across 18 fields** covering customer demographics, purchase details, and shopping behavior.

The analysis aims to:

* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify high-value customer segments

The dataset contains **3,900 customer purchase transactions and 18 columns**.

### Customer Information

* Location

### Purchase Information

* Item Purchased
* Category
* Purchase Amount
* Season
* Size
* Color

### Shopping Behavior

* Discount Applied
* Promo Code Used
* Previous Purchases
* Frequency of Purchases
* Review Rating
* Shipping Type

The original dataset contained **37 missing values in the Review Rating column**. 

---

#  1. Python — Data Preparation & Exploratory Analysis

Python was used as the first stage of the analytical workflow to understand, clean, and prepare the raw dataset.

### Tools

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

### Data Exploration

The dataset was initially inspected to understand:

* Dataset structure
* Column data types
* Summary statistics
* Numerical distributions
* Categorical variables
* Missing values
* Data consistency

Pandas functions such as `info()` and `describe()` were used during the initial exploration. 

### Data Cleaning

The following preparation steps were performed:

* Identified missing values
* Imputed missing `review_rating` values using the **median rating of the corresponding product category**
* Standardized column names using `snake_case`
* Checked data consistency
* Identified redundancy between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used`
* Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` field

### Feature Engineering

Two additional analytical features were created:

* `age_group` — customer ages grouped into meaningful age categories
* `purchase_frequency_days` — derived from purchase-frequency information

The cleaned DataFrame was then loaded into **PostgreSQL** for structured SQL analysis. 

---

#  2. PostgreSQL & SQL Analysis

After cleaning, the dataset was integrated into **PostgreSQL** to perform structured business analysis.

The SQL analysis was designed around **10 business questions** covering revenue, customers, products, discounts, subscriptions, and purchasing behavior. 

### Business Questions

#### 1. Revenue by Gender

Compare total revenue generated by male and female customers.

#### 2. High-Spending Discount Users

Identify customers who use discounts while still spending above the average purchase amount.

#### 3. Top 5 Products by Rating

Identify products with the highest average customer review ratings.

#### 4. Shipping Type Comparison

Compare average purchase amounts between different shipping methods, particularly Standard and Express shipping.

#### 5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers

Compare spending and revenue between customers with and without subscriptions.

#### 6. Discount-Dependent Products

Identify products with the highest percentage of purchases made using discounts.

#### 7. Customer Segmentation

Classify customers into:

* **New**
* **Returning**
* **Loyal**

based on their purchase history.

#### 8. Top 3 Products per Category

Identify the three most-purchased products within each product category.

#### 9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions

Analyze whether customers with more than five purchases are more likely to have an active subscription.

#### 10. Revenue by Age Group

Measure the contribution of different customer age groups to total revenue. 

---

#  Customer Segmentation

Customer purchase history was used to divide customers into three behavioral segments:

| Segment       | Description                                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **New**       | Customers making their first purchases            |
| **Returning** | Customers who have made repeat purchases          |
| **Loyal**     | Customers with a higher level of purchase history |

The analysis found that customers with **more than five purchases are more likely to have an active subscription**, while the Loyal segment shows the highest spending and strongest subscription rate. 

This segmentation provides a foundation for differentiated customer engagement and marketing strategies.

---

#  Discount & Purchasing Behavior

The analysis examined whether discounts are being used primarily to encourage purchases or are also being applied to customers who already demonstrate strong purchasing behavior.

One important finding was that **above-average spenders still make use of discounts**, suggesting that some discounts may be reaching customers who already demonstrate strong demand. 

The analysis also identified a small group of products with a particularly high proportion of discounted purchases, indicating potential **discount dependency**. 

---

#  Product Analysis

Product performance was examined using:

* Average review rating
* Purchase volume
* Product category
* Repeat purchasing behavior

The analysis identified a set of highly rated products and the **top three products within each category**.

These products provide useful signals for merchandising and campaign positioning. 

---

#  Shipping Behavior

Shipping type was analyzed to understand differences in customer spending behavior.

The analysis found that **average purchase amounts differ between Standard and Express shipping customers**, indicating that shipping preference can be useful when examining customer purchasing segments.

---

#  Key Business Insights

The combined Python, SQL, and Power BI analysis produced several important findings.

### Subscription Behavior

Subscribers demonstrate **higher average spending and higher total revenue** than non-subscribers. Customers with more than five purchases are also more likely to hold an active subscription. 

### Customer Loyalty

Purchase history provides a meaningful basis for distinguishing New, Returning, and Loyal customers. The Loyal segment represents customers with the strongest spending and subscription behavior. 

### Discounts

Some high-spending customers continue to use discounts, while certain products show strong dependence on discounted purchases. 

### Product Performance

A relatively clear group of products leads in average ratings and repeat purchasing behavior. The analysis also identifies leading products within each category. 

### Customer Demographics

Revenue was analyzed across different age groups to identify the customer cohorts contributing to overall revenue. 

---

#  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project proposes five strategic actions:

### 1. Boost Subscriptions

Promote exclusive benefits and incentives to encourage customers to subscribe, particularly among repeat buyers.

### 2. Build Customer Loyalty Programs

Use loyalty programs to encourage Returning customers to progress toward the Loyal segment.

### 3. Review Discount Policy

Evaluate discount usage among high-spending customers and discount-dependent products to better balance sales volume and margin.

### 4. Sharpen Product Positioning

Use highly rated and best-selling products as focal points in merchandising and marketing campaigns.

### 5. Target Customers by Segment

Use customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and shipping preferences to make marketing efforts more targeted.

These recommendations are derived from the project's SQL findings and dashboard analysis.
